Zelvarix

Zelvarix is a B2B contact discovery tool for small and medium sales teams, with market search, direct contact reveal, and AI insights on each prospect.

What it does

  • Market search: filter by industry, company name, company keyword, company size, seniority, department, state and city, or search by a person's name.
  • Direct contact reveal: email and phone stay hidden until you choose to reveal a contact.
  • AI insights: ice breakers, a score and a draft email for each prospect.

Zelvarix covers US contacts only.
