Why ZoomInfo doesn't fit a small team

ZoomInfo is a genuinely powerful platform, built for enterprise go-to-market orgs with dedicated revenue-ops teams. Three things make it a poor fit below that size:

  • No public pricing, no self-serve signup. You talk to sales, get a custom quote, and negotiate. There's no way to just start using it today.
  • Five-figure entry point. Publicly reported buyer data puts the Professional plan around $14,995 a year before seats, with a median contract near $33,500 a year across all tiers.
  • Mandatory annual contracts. Plans are sold as 12-month commitments, commonly with auto-renewal and a narrow cancellation window before renewal. If the tool isn't right for your team, you're locked in either way.
  • Seats and credits are separate line items. Additional users run roughly $1,500–$2,500 a year each on top of the platform fee, and data credits are pooled and can run out mid-contract.

None of that is a flaw in ZoomInfo. It's a pricing model built for a buyer with a procurement process and a six-figure tools budget — which most teams of 2 to 10 don't have.
